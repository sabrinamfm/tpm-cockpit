# Operational Intelligence

## Principle

TPM Cockpit should help turn scattered operational information into usable awareness.

Operational intelligence means understanding:

- what is happening
- what changed
- what is blocked
- what is at risk
- what needs follow-up
- what decisions are pending

## Phase 1 Interpretation

In Phase 1, operational intelligence does not mean advanced automation.

It means the system keeps related information organized enough that the TPM can reason clearly about the program.

## Product Direction

The cockpit should eventually help generate reports, surface risks, and identify stale coordination patterns.

But first, it must capture the basic operational truth.
