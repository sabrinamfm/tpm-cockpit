# Morning Routine

## Purpose

The morning routine is the daily entry point into TPM Cockpit.

It should help the TPM quickly understand what requires attention today.

## Questions to Answer

The cockpit should help answer:

- What is blocked?
- What is overdue?
- What changed recently?
- What dependencies need follow-up?
- What decisions are pending?
- What risks need attention?
- What should I communicate today?

## Phase 1 Workflow

A TPM opens the cockpit and reviews:

1. Programs in progress
2. Attention items
3. Blocked work items
4. Stale dependencies
5. Open risks
6. Pending decisions
7. Notes or updates from the previous day

## Design Direction

The morning routine should be fast.

The goal is not to review everything. The goal is to know where to focus.
